Lassagna Recipe

Description:

Layers of tender pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and savory herbs baked to golden perfection—an Italian comfort food classic.

Ingredients:

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 500g ground beef (or Italian sausage)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 800g tomato sauce or crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 400g ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • ½ cup Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (optional)

Steps:

  1. Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions, then drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef and onion over medium heat until browned. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Stir in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, egg, and parsley until well mixed.
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a baking dish.
  7. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  8. Top with the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  9. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  10. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 20–25 minutes, or until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
  11. Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing and serving.
