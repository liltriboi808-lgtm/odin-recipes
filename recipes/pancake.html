Pancake Recipe

Description:

Light, fluffy, and golden brown, these classic pancakes make the perfect breakfast treat topped with syrup, fruit, or butter.

Ingredients:

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 3½ tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1¼ cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)
  • Butter or oil for cooking
  • Maple syrup, honey, or fresh fruit for serving (optional)
