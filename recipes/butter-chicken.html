Butter Chicken Recipe

Description:

Tender chicken simmered in a rich, creamy tomato-based sauce infused with aromatic Indian spices.

Ingredients:

  • 500g boneless chicken, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 400g tomato puree
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)

Steps:

  1. In a bowl, combine the yogurt, lemon juice, turmeric, red chili powder, and garam masala. Add the chicken and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil and 1 tablespoon of butter in a large pan over medium heat.
  3. Add the marinated chicken and cook until lightly browned. Remove and set aside.
  4. In the same pan, melt the remaining butter and sauté the onion until soft and golden.
  5. Add the garlic and ginger, and cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
  6. Stir in the tomato puree, cumin powder, coriander powder, and salt. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Return the chicken to the pan and mix well with the sauce.
  8. Pour in the heavy cream and stir until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
  9. Simmer for another 10–15 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
  10. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve hot with naan, roti, or steamed rice.
